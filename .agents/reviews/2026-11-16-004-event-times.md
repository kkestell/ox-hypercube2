## F1. Mock failed run ends before its agent's messages were sent

Low · Correctness · Not fixed · `ui/mock/scenarios.ts:119`

In the `run-status` mock scenario, `Listing.run` ticks the failed run's `ended_at` when the run is built, a minute after its `created_at`. The scenario's messages are ticked afterwards, the running run's first and then the failed run's. The failed run's notice therefore shows a time several minutes earlier than the two messages above it, the opposite of what a real run produces. This affects only the mock that is used to look at the gutter layout.

### Evidence

`runStatus` builds the running run (created at tick 1), then the failed run (created at tick 2, `ended_at` at tick 3), then `text(...)` ticks 4 to 7 for the four messages, the failed run's at ticks 6 and 7.

### Fix

None. Recorded as a Low finding and left unchanged.
